# Quantum Stats

{% hint style="warning" %}
Quantum Stats is in private development. Installation, supported Unity
versions, exported packages, and online compatibility will be documented only
after the module passes its release proof.
{% endhint %}

Quantum Stats keeps character stats inside Photon Quantum's deterministic
simulation while letting Game Creator 2 author catalogs, send commands, and
present a read-only mirror.

Planned gameplay support includes:

- fixed-point Stats and Attributes;
- additive and multiplicative modifiers;
- deterministic regeneration;
- authoritative damage and healing;
- stable Status Effect identity, duration, stacking, refresh, expiry, and
  removal;
- predicted feedback and verified presentation;
- rollback, late join, and reconnect.

Photon Quantum and Game Creator packages are installed separately. They are not
included with Quantum Stats.

Continue with [Deterministic Architecture](deterministic-architecture.md) or
review why [Project Settings](project-settings.md) are currently not applicable.
