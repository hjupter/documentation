# Conditions

* **Is Member In Faction** — whether a target has joined a specific Faction.
* **Is Faction Status** — whether one Faction has the selected status toward
  another Faction.
* **Is Member Status** — compares one Member's resolved status toward another.
* **Is Member Towards Faction** — compares a Member's resolved status toward a
  Faction.

All conditions return false for a missing required Member or Faction instead of
throwing an exception.
